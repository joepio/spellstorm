Spellstorm is couch co-op for one to six wizards. Weave eight elements into
thousands of spells and descend through five biomes together.

- Five big levels, each a winding way through three zones, with shrines of
  tomes on the way and a boss at the end.
- Eighteen kinds of monster, each with its own attack, weakness and place
  to come from.
- Plant traps and sentries, enchant your staff, or knock bullets back with your staff.

Windows x86-64, portable: unzip and run Spellstorm.exe. Works best with
controllers; with GameNight, phones and seats are handled for you.
